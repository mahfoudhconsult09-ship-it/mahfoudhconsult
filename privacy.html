Privacy

Draft: review before relying on this

This page explains what happens to the information you give this site. I'm Mahfoudh Musa, and you can reach me at mahfoudhdigital006@gmail.com.

Forms

The contact form collects your name, email address, an optional store link and your message. The audit report form collects your email address and the store address you scanned. I use this only to reply to you.

Netlify processes form submissions for this site.

The free audit

When you run the audit, the store address you enter is sent to Google's PageSpeed service, which runs the scan. Google handles that request under its own policies.

Third-party services on this site

  • The Instagram feed on the home page is provided by Behold and loads content from Instagram.
  • The voice assistant is provided by Fish Audio. If you use it, your voice is processed by that service.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts. Store logos and previews on the Work page load through Google's favicon service and Microlink.

These services may set their own cookies or collect technical data such as your IP address. I do not control how they do it.

Your choices

Write to me and I will delete a message you sent. You can also choose not to use the forms, the audit or the voice assistant.
