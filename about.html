The short story

I study Computer Science at Yakubu Gowon University of Abuja. Alongside my degree I build tools people can use, and I run marketing for online stores.

Almost every product I've built began as something I kept watching go wrong: sellers losing track of orders, photographers forgetting follow-ups, shoppers unable to find stores that ship to them. I build a first version, put it live, and fix what real use shows me.

Marketing taught me the other half. A good product with no traffic is invisible, so I learned Pinterest, Google Shopping, Search Console and Klaviyo by working on real stores.

Now I'm going deeper into AI and machine learning, because the best tools I can build are ones that learn from data.
