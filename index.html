SEOM LIVE

Search for products across countries and see at a glance what is in stock and what ships to you. Store owners can paste their link and get a free e-commerce and SEO audit.

  • Country filter: Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, South Africa, the UK, the US, Canada, Australia
  • Discovers public store and product pages automatically
  • Audit covers discoverability, trust signals, mobile UX and SEO basics

The problem: Good stores are hard to find, and shoppers can't tell what actually ships to them.

Open SEOM →

TrackFlow LIVE

Order tracking for small online stores. Create an order, send the customer a tracking link on WhatsApp, and move it through new, preparing, out for delivery and delivered.

  • One tap sends the link on WhatsApp
  • Works with your own rider or any courier, nothing to integrate
  • One dashboard for every order's status

The problem: Small sellers run delivery through chat threads and answer "where is my order?" all day.

Open TrackFlow →

PhotoFlow LIVE

A lead tracker for photographers. Every inquiry moves through a pipeline until it becomes a booking, and the dashboard tells you who to follow up with today.

  • Stages: new, contacted, quote sent, follow-up, booked, lost
  • Tracks session type, event date, estimated value and lead source
  • A daily "follow up today" list

The problem: Bookings slip away when a follow-up is forgotten.

Open PhotoFlow →

PolyLean LIVE

Puts live Polymarket odds next to independent forecasts, then suggests Yes, No or Skip. It only recommends an action when the chance is high and the price is favorable.

  • You set the minimum chance before it suggests anything
  • Shows the sources behind each forecast
  • Keeps its own scorecard, and an optional bankroll input to size stakes

The problem: Prediction-market prices are easy to read and hard to judge. Information only, not financial advice.

Open PolyLean →

Qur'an Daily LIVE

A calm place for the Qur'an. Read all 114 surahs in Uthmani script, listen to recitation that plays while you read, and follow verified reciters.

  • Multiple Qira'at and Riwayat, adjustable text size
  • Reciters apply, submit a trial recitation, and are reviewed before publishing
  • Accounts, uploads, supporter tools. Built end to end on Floot

The problem: Reading, listening and reciter publishing usually live in separate apps.

Open Qur'an Daily →

How I work

Three rules I don't break.

Measure before building

I start from your real data: Search Console, your store analytics, an audit of the site. Opinions come second.

Ship small, learn from use

A first version goes live fast. Then real people break it, and I fix what they actually hit.

Show my sources

When I quote a number from Google or Pinterest, I label it as their benchmark, never as my result.

Free tool

What would a crawler say about your store?

Stores quietly lose traffic on small things: a returns page Google expects, product data Shopping can't read, a tag nobody installed. Paste your address and I crawl up to ten pages and score six layers. You see the weakest plate first, with the fix.
