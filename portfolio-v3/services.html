What I do

Eight services. One goal: your product gets found and bought.

Pinterest marketing

People use Pinterest to plan what to buy, so your products can be found before they ever search on Google.

  • Catalog and product feed setup
  • Pinterest Tag plus Conversions API, so sales are attributed properly
  • Pin and board creative with keyword-rich titles

You get: an approved feed, working tracking and a pin plan you can run.

Pinterest reports 28% higher ROAS with brand keywords in titles, and 23.7% more attributed conversions with Tag plus Conversions API. Their benchmark, not my result.

Google Shopping

Shopping ads and free listings only show products whose data Google can read and trust.

  • Merchant Center feed cleanup: title order, GTINs, price and stock accuracy
  • Fix diagnostics and disapprovals
  • Shopping and Performance Max campaigns once the feed is clean

You get: a feed Google accepts and campaigns you can read.

Google reports about 20% more clicks on average when products have GTINs. Their benchmark, not my result.

SEO and Search Console

Search Console tells you exactly what Google sees: impressions, clicks, position, and what it can't crawl.

  • Technical fixes: sitemap, canonicals, speed, structured data
  • Product and collection page copy written for search and for people
  • A monthly Search Console review in plain language

You get: a ranked fix list, then monthly proof of what moved.

Email with Klaviyo

Email is the one channel you own. Flows keep earning while you do other work.

  • Welcome, abandoned-cart and post-purchase flows
  • Segments and campaigns tied to real purchase data
  • 1 to 2 years of hands-on Klaviyo experience

You get: flows that run on their own and a clear read on revenue per recipient.

Multi-channel strategy

Pinterest, Google, email and content should feed each other, not compete.

  • Tracking installed first so every channel can be measured
  • One plan that shows which channel earns what
  • Monthly review against your own dashboards

You get: a single plan instead of five disconnected tactics.

AI agents and automation

The repetitive work in a store is the easiest thing to hand to software.

  • Voice and chat assistants, like the one on this site
  • Audit and research crawlers that read sites and report
  • Small automations around orders, leads and reports

You get: a tool that does one job reliably, with a link you can open.

Websites and stores

A fast, clear site is what every other channel sends people to.

  • Shopify, WordPress or hand-coded HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • Mobile-first, accessible, clean structure for search
  • Scroll and 3D design when it helps tell the story

You get: a site you can edit yourself and that loads fast on a phone.

Graphics and video

Ads and posts need creative that matches the offer.

  • Ad creatives and product graphics (I work in Canva)
  • Short video edits for social and ads
  • Beginner in motion design, improving with every project

You get: ready-to-post assets sized for each channel.
