Privacy

Draft: review before relying on this

This page explains what happens to the information you give this site. I'm Mahfoudh Musa. Reach me at mahfoudhdigital006@gmail.com.

Forms

The contact form collects your name, email, an optional website link and your message. The audit report form collects your email and the site you audited. I use this only to reply to you. Netlify processes form submissions.

The free audit

The site address you enter is sent to my audit service, which fetches your public pages, robots.txt, sitemap and llms.txt to score them. I do not store the result. If a Google PageSpeed key is configured, the address is also sent to Google's PageSpeed service.

Third-party services

  • The voice assistant is provided by Fish Audio. It loads only after you first interact with the page. If you use it, your voice is processed by that service.
  • Fonts are hosted on this site.

These services may set their own cookies or collect technical data such as your IP address.

Your choices

Write to me and I will delete a message you sent. You can choose not to use the forms, the audit or the voice assistant.
